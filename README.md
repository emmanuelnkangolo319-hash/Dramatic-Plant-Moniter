# Dramatic-Plant-Moniter
A plant monitor that makes weird sounds and faces to show when you need to water your plant.

Im making this product so people can connect to their plants abit more, i think replacing the job removes the whole point of getting your own plant. It makes weird sounds and shows how it feels because i wanted it to bring the emotions of the plant to life.
